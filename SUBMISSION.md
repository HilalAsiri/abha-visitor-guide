Project option: Abha Visitor Guide
Problem: Friends visiting Abha need a simple, reliable way to discover nearby places by interest and get directions without a paid map API key.
What I built: A bilingual Arabic-English, mobile-first interactive Abha visitor map using Leaflet and OpenStreetMap. It filters verified locations by category, shows place cards, supports RTL and light/dark mode, and opens the marker coordinates in Google Maps.
Result: Visitors can choose a category, explore 15 mapped places around Abha, Al Soudah, and Rijal Almaa, and open the selected point in Google Maps for directions.
